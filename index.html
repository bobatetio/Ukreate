Common Questions

Everything you need to know about Ukreate

How does Ukreate work for hotels? +

Hotels create a profile, set their campaign brief, and browse our vetted creator network. Once you find the right match, Ukreate handles contracts, scheduling, and content delivery — all in one platform.

How are creators vetted on Ukreate? +

Every creator goes through a multi-step review covering content quality, audience authenticity, engagement rates, and a track record of professional hotel collaborations. Less than 10% of applicants are accepted.

What content formats do creators produce? +

Creators on Ukreate produce UGC videos, Instagram Reels, TikToks, YouTube vlogs, photography packages, and native content for your owned channels — all tailored to your brief.

How much does a creator collaboration cost? +

Collaboration costs vary by creator tier and deliverables. Our Starter plan includes 3 projects per month from $1,950/year. Enterprise and custom packages are available for larger hotel groups.

Who handles contracts and licensing? +

Ukreate manages all contracts, NDAs, usage rights, and licensing agreements on your behalf. You retain full usage rights to the content produced under your campaigns.

How long does a typical campaign take? +

From brief to published content, most campaigns complete in 2–4 weeks. Rush timelines are available on Growth and Enterprise plans.

Can I choose which creators I work with? +

Yes. You can browse creator profiles, review their portfolio and metrics, and select the exact creators you want — or let our matching algorithm suggest the best fit for your campaign goals.
